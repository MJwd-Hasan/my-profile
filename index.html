Explainable ML for Child Malnutrition

Identifying children at high risk of severe malnutrition in Bangladesh

An epidemiological case study of severe wasting among infants aged 6–23 months using nationwide MICS-25 data. Addresses missing-data mechanisms and survey-weighted distributions, with machine learning and SHAP to investigate risk drivers. Accepted at the 2nd International Student Research Conference 2026.
